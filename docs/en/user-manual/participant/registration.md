# Registration

You apply right on the conference's dashboard. It holds a single form that takes you through your application step by step. Once you send it, the dashboard turns into your application, which you complete before the application deadline.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

- **Account:** Sign in with **Sign in** at the top right. The first time, DELEGATOR asks you under **My Account**, with the notice **Complete data**, for your personal details: name, phone number, emergency contacts, address, birth date, gender and diet. After **Save** you continue where you were heading. You must be at least 13 years old. What happens to these details is explained under [Your account](./account#profile).
- **Finding the conference:** **Conferences** lists every conference, grouped by its stage. The ones under **Registration open** are the ones you can apply for right now. Open one to reach its dashboard.
- **One part per conference:** You take part in a conference in exactly one way: as a delegation member, on your own, as a supervisor or on the team. If you already have a part in it, DELEGATOR refuses a second one.
- **Conference language:** Above the form it says which language the conference is held in, for example **The conference is held in German**. Debates, speeches and papers are in that language. Only apply if you speak it well enough.
- **Age:** A conference can have age limits, measured on its first day. If your age does not fit, the form shows **About your age**. As a delegation member or single participant your application then cannot be sent. If you are too old to take part but old enough to supervise, **Apply as a supervisor** switches you straight to signing up as a supervisor. If you have questions about the limits, the team will help.

You only see the application form while you have no part in the conference and registration is open. Otherwise the dashboard shows **You are not taking part** and tells you whether registration has not opened yet or is already closed. If the conference is already **In preparation**, it offers **Join the waiting list now** instead (see [The waiting list](#the-waiting-list)).

## Choosing how to take part {#choosing-how-to-take-part}

In the **Sign up** section, the first step, **Choose a path**, offers four ways in:

![The four ways to apply on a conference's dashboard](shot:participant/registration-choice)

- **Create delegation:** You found a delegation, lead it and invite the others. For a state or a non-state actor you apply as a group of at least two people.
- **Join delegation:** Someone has already founded a delegation and given you a code.
- **Individual application:** You apply on your own for a special role, such as the conference press.
- **Supervisor:** You accompany your students as a teacher or club leader.

The steps of the path you picked then open below, one after the other. A finished step gets a check mark and only shows what you chose. **Edit** opens it again. Nothing is sent until you click the button in the last step.

Not sure which path fits you? **Start assistant** asks you a few questions and leads you to the right one. **How do I apply?** shows the same as a diagram. **Seats of the Conference** shows the conference's committees and their agenda items, its states and its non-state actors.

:::tip Older links
Links like `/registration/<id>/create-delegation`, or a join link with a code, still work. They lead to the conference's dashboard and open the form on the matching path.
:::

## Creating a delegation {#creating-a-delegation}

1. Choose **Create delegation**. The **Info** step briefly explains what that means: you apply together, you become the head delegate and invite the others with a code. If you are on your own or only want to supervise, you can switch straight to **Individual application** or **Apply as Supervisor** from here. Otherwise click **Next**.
2. **School:** Pick your school from the suggestions or type its full name. You may also leave the field empty.
3. **Motivation:** Why is your delegation applying to the conference? Also explain why you want to represent the states or roles you wish for.
4. **Experience:** How much experience do you have with simulation games like Model United Nations?
5. Click **Create delegation**.

Motivation and experience are part of the selection process, so careful answers improve your chances. You can leave them empty for now and fill them in on the dashboard later.

Your delegation now exists, and the dashboard shows what is still open under **Your application**. Depending on the conference's settings you also get an email with the code to pass on.

- **Invite people:** A delegation needs at least two members. Under **Delegation Members**, **Invite more people** shows your code. You can copy it (**Copy code**), send a link (**Copy join link**) or show a QR code (**Show QR code**). As head delegate, **Rotate Code** gives you a new code. The old one stops working, and whoever has already joined stays in the delegation.
- **Enter delegation preferences:** **Set Preferences** lets you pick at least three states or non-state actors you would like to represent, and put them in your order with the arrows. Only roles with enough seats for all members are offered. If your delegation grows, preferences that no longer fit are dropped.
- **Answer questionnaire:** Motivation and experience under **Information and Motivation** have to be filled in.
- **Complete the profiles and fit the age range:** This applies to everyone in the delegation (see [What holds an application back](#application-blockers)).

As head delegate you can hand the lead to someone else with **Make Head Delegate**. You then become a regular member without special rights. **Remove member** takes a member out of the delegation. Everyone names their own school, and you can change yours at any time.

When someone joins, every member who was there before can get an email with the new person's name, depending on the conference's settings.

### What holds an application back {#application-blockers}

An application can only be sent once everyone's profile is complete and everyone fits the conference's age range. If something is missing, **Your application** shows the box **What still holds the application back** with every person concerned and what is wrong, for example **profile incomplete** with the missing fields, or **too young for the conference**.

- If it is your own profile, **Complete data** takes you to **My Account**. After **Save** you come back.
- If it is someone else's, it says **Only they can complete their own profile.** Let them know.
- If you have questions about the age range, contact the team.

### Completing the application {#completing-the-application}

Once every task is done, the head delegate completes the application with **Complete registration and participate in the selection process** and confirms the question that follows. Until then the button is locked. Every other member sees **Only the head delegate can complete the application, once every task is done.** in its place.

Afterwards it says **Registration Successfully Completed**. Only completed applications take part in the selection process. Depending on the conference's settings, every member gets a confirmation by email.

:::warning Complete it in time
The deadline is shown right under **Your application**. A delegation that has not completed its application by then is not considered in the selection process. A few days before the deadline the conference can remind everyone whose application is still open by email.
:::

After completing it you cannot change anything: no preferences or texts, no new members, and nobody can leave or delete the delegation any more. Only your own school can still be changed, and you can still connect supervisors.

You see the result of the selection process on the dashboard once the team has published the assignment. Depending on the conference's settings you also get it by email. What happens on the dashboard after that is described under [Dashboard](./dashboard#after-applying).

## Joining a delegation {#joining-a-delegation}

To join, you need the code from your head delegate. It has eight characters, and older six-character codes still work. If you got a join link, the code is already filled in.

1. Choose **Join delegation**.
2. Type the code in the **Enter code** step.
3. Check the details: conference, school, head delegate and number of members.
4. **Which school/institution do you come from?** is filled in with the head delegate's school. Change it if you come from another one, or leave it empty.
5. Click **Confirm**.

If the code matches no delegation, you see **No delegations found**. After ten wrong codes in ten minutes you have to wait a few minutes. If the delegation has already completed its application, DELEGATOR refuses with **Delegation has already applied, joins are not possible anymore**.

After joining, your dashboard shows the delegation's application. Depending on the conference's settings you get an email confirming that you joined. As a member you can see the whole application, but only the head delegate can change and complete it.

## Applying as a single participant {#applying-as-a-single-participant}

Individual applications are for special roles that are not tied to a delegation, for example the conference press. For a state you always apply as a delegation.

1. Choose **Individual application**.
2. In the **Choose a role** step, pick a role and click **Choose this role**.
3. Name your **School** (optional) and answer **Motivation** and **Experience**. In the motivation, also explain your choice of role.
4. Click **Individual application**.

On the dashboard, **Add another application** under **Role Applications** lets you apply for more roles. The bin next to a role withdraws that one application. The last role cannot be removed that way, that is what **Delete all applications** is for (see [Withdrawing an application](#withdrawing-an-application)). If you are not accepted for any of your roles, the team will contact you and suggest another role.

Just like a delegation, you complete your application yourself. Your tasks are **Apply for one (or more) roles**, **Answer questionnaire** and **Complete the profiles and fit the age range**. The button, the deadline and the emails are the same as for [completing a delegation's application](#completing-the-application).

## Registering as a supervisor {#registering-as-a-supervisor}

As a supervisor you accompany your students, for example as a teacher or club leader. You see their applications and status and can help them with the organisation. You do not get a seat of your own, so there is no application to complete.

1. Choose **Supervisor**.
2. Under **School / Institution**, say where you are from. It is optional and helps the team look after a school's delegations together. Click **Next**.
3. Under **Attendance**, choose whether you come along (**I'm coming along**, which includes paying the participation fee and sending documents of your own) or only accompany the preparation (**I supervise from afar**).
4. Click **Register as supervisor**. You then land on your dashboard.

**I'm coming along** requires a complete profile and the conference's minimum age for supervisors. Otherwise the form shows **What still keeps you from attending in person**, and you can only sign up with **I supervise from afar**. You change whether you come along on the dashboard under **Your details**, but only while registration is open.

Delegations can send you a link of their own (**Copy supervisor link**). It opens the **Registration for Supervisors** page with the same steps. Through this link you can still sign up after the application deadline, until the conference is over. After that it says **Supervisor sign-up is closed**.

### Connecting with your students {#connecting-with-your-students}

For privacy reasons you cannot add your students yourself. They connect with you:

1. On your dashboard, **Connect with students** shows your code and a link with a QR code you can send. **Rotate Code** gives you a new code. The old code and your old link stop working, and whoever is already connected stays so.
2. Your students enter the code on their dashboard under **Supervisors**, at **Connect Supervisor**, or open your link, which fills the code in for them.
3. They see your name and school and click **Connect**.

Delegation members and single participants of the conference can connect, also after completing their application. A wrong code shows **Not found**, and after ten wrong codes in ten minutes they have to wait a little. Depending on the conference's settings you get an email for every newly connected person, and the person learns by email that you supervise them.

:::caution Check before connecting
Only participant care can undo a connection, and once connected the supervisor sees the person's data and status. If the supervisor is at a different school, DELEGATOR points that out before connecting.
:::

## The waiting list {#the-waiting-list}

The waiting list is open while the conference is **In preparation**. The conference's dashboard then offers **Join the waiting list now**:

- if you have no part in the conference yet, under **You are not taking part**,
- if you applied and did not get a seat, under **Application Rejected**, once the team has released the assignment.

To put yourself on it:

1. On the **Vacancies / Waiting List** page, fill in the **Questionnaire**: your school (optional), your motivation and your experience.
2. If you have special requests about your role or committee, add them. Every restriction lowers your chances, so leave the field empty for the best ones.
3. Click **Save**.

The page then confirms that you are on the waiting list, and depending on the conference's settings you get a confirmation by email. You change your details with the same form. **Leave the waiting list** gives up your place on it, which is lost once you confirm.

Participant care gives out the seats from the waiting list first come, first served, and contacts you when a seat becomes free. If you get one, your dashboard shows your new role, your school from the waiting list is carried over, and depending on the conference's settings you get an email with your role.

DELEGATOR refuses an entry if

- the conference is not in preparation (**The waiting list for this conference is not open.**),
- you are already on it (**You are already on the waiting list for this conference.**),
- you applied and the assignment has not been released yet (**You can join the waiting list once the assignment has been released.**),
- you already have a seat (**You already have a place at this conference and cannot join the waiting list.**).

## Withdrawing an application {#withdrawing-an-application}

As long as your application is not completed, you can withdraw it under **Danger Zone** on the dashboard. Each action asks first and then takes you to the conference overview.

- **Leave Delegation:** You leave the delegation, as long as others are still in it. You cannot come back without the code. If the head delegate leaves, another member takes over.
- **Delete Delegation:** The head delegate deletes the whole delegation, with every member's registration. This cannot be undone.
- **Delete all applications:** You withdraw your individual application with all its roles. To undo it, you have to apply again.

While registration is open, you can then apply again another way. Once the application is completed, you can no longer do this yourself, and the **Danger Zone** tells you to contact participant care. Give them the ID shown at the bottom of the **Danger Zone**. Participant care can withdraw an application at any stage.
