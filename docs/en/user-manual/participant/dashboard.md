# Your conference dashboard

A conference's dashboard holds everything about your participation: your application, your role, the formalities still open and, at the end, your certificate. What you see depends on how far along the conference is and how you take part (in a delegation, as a single participant or as a supervisor).

:::tip Guided tour
The help button (the question mark in the header) offers a guided tour of this page.
:::

## At a glance {#overview}

The conference header with its title, dates and stage is always at the top. Below it, the content changes with the stage:

- **Registration:** your application, your delegation's members, your delegation preferences and your texts.
- **After the deadline:** the notice **Selection process in progress**, then **The assignment is still in progress** until the team releases the assignment.
- **Preparation:** your role, your **Personal Status** with payment and postal registration, the **Quick Links** and the surveys.
- **During the conference:** the same, plus your digital nametag and the check-in.
- **After the conference:** your **Certificate of participation**.

If you are not taking part in the conference yet, you see the application instead. How that works is described in [Registration](./registration#choosing-how-to-take-part).

## While registration is open {#while-registration-is-open}

### Your application

The **Your application** section shows what is still open and the **Application deadline**. In a delegation there are three tasks:

- **Invite people:** a delegation needs at least 2 members.
- **Enter delegation preferences:** at least 3 countries or non-state actors.
- **Answer questionnaire:** the questions about motivation and experience.

Once all are done, the head delegate completes the application with **Complete registration and participate in the selection process**. After that, nothing can be changed.

:::warning Complete it in time
Only completed applications take part in the selection process. Having created a delegation is not enough.
:::

### Delegation members and entry code {#delegation-members}

Under **Delegation Members** you see every member and the auto-generated **Delegation Name**. Mention this name when you talk to participant care.

As long as the application is not completed, this is also where the entry code is, which others use to join your delegation. Next to it you can:

- **Copy code** or **Copy join link** to pass either on,
- **Copy supervisor link** so your supervisors can register,
- **Show QR code** so others can scan the link directly,
- **Rotate Code** (head delegate only) if the code got into the wrong hands.

What joining looks like from the other side is described in [Joining a delegation](./registration#joining-a-delegation).

### The head delegate

Whoever creates the delegation is its head delegate at first. Only the head delegate can edit the preferences and the texts and complete the application. Until then they can also **Make Head Delegate** or **Remove member** for any member. Everyone else sees the same sections but can only read them.

### Delegation preferences

Under **Delegation Preferences** the head delegate uses **Set Preferences** to choose from the **Nations Pool** and the **Non-State Actors**. Only roles with enough seats for every member of your delegation are offered. You can change the order of your preferences with the arrows.

### Information and motivation

Here you answer why your delegation is applying and how much experience you have with Model United Nations. Above that, everyone gives their own **School / Institution** (see [Your school](#school)).

### Supervisors

If you take part with supervisors, such as a teacher, you see them under **Supervisors**. With **Connect Supervisor** you enter their invitation code, check the data shown and click **Connect**. Only participant care can undo the connection. If you opened a link from your supervisor, the dashboard scrolls to this section and highlights it. The code is already filled in, so you only check the data and click **Connect**.

### Danger Zone

Until the application is completed you can **Leave Delegation** here (if there is more than one of you). The head delegate can also **Delete Delegation**. Your delegation ID is here too, in case support asks for it.

### As a single participant

If you apply alone for an individual role, your dashboard looks similar. Your tasks are **Apply for one (or more) roles** and **Answer questionnaire**. Under **Role Applications** you add more roles with **Add another application**. In the **Danger Zone** you can withdraw with **Delete all applications**. You complete the application yourself. More in [Applying as a single participant](./registration#applying-as-a-single-participant).

## After applying {#after-applying}

Once the application deadline has passed, the dashboard shows **Selection process in progress**. Below it is either **Application completed** (you take part in the selection process) or **Application not completed** (you cannot be considered). A completed application does not guarantee a seat.

When the team has handed out the seats but not released them yet, you see **The assignment is still in progress**. Only with the release do you learn which role you get. You will be notified by email, so there is no need to ask.

![Dashboard while the team assigns the seats](shot:participant/dashboard-assignment-pending)

### Not accepted {#not-accepted}

If your application did not get a seat, you see **Application Rejected**. While the conference is being prepared or running, you can put yourself on the waiting list below it with **Join the waiting list now**. Seats from there are given out first come, first served. More in [The waiting list](./registration#the-waiting-list).

## During preparation {#preparation}

With the release you see whom you represent. This is a head delegate's dashboard with everything unlocked:

![A head delegate's dashboard during preparation](shot:participant/dashboard-preparation)

- **Quick Links:** cards for everything there is to do now. Cards marked **!** still need something from you. Greyed out cards have not been unlocked by the team yet.
- **Personal Status:** your formal steps: **Registration**, **Fee Payment**, **Binding Registration**, for minors the **Parental Agreement**, the **Media Usage Agreement** and open surveys. You cannot take part without all of them. Each step is **Completed**, **Pending**, **Problem** or **Not unlocked yet**. It stays pending until participant care has ticked it off.
- **Delegation Status:** the country or non-state actor your delegation represents and the committees you sit in.
- **Delegation Members:** every member with their committee, payment and registration status.
- **Information on your country** or **Information on your NSA**.

As a single participant you see your **Role** instead of the delegation status and members.

When paper submission is open, the **Paper Hub** card takes you there. That is where you submit position, working and introduction papers and read everyone else's.

## Payment {#payment}

The **Fee Payment** card opens the payment page once the team unlocks payments. You pay the participation fee by bank transfer:

1. If you are in a delegation or a supervisor, **Pay for others too** lets you cover several people with one transfer and select them.
2. Click **Generate Transaction Reference**.
3. Transfer the amount with exactly the **Reason** shown. Scanning the **GiroCode** with your banking app is easiest.

This reference is the only way the team can match your payment. Once it has arrived, the page says **Your fee is paid** and the card disappears from the quick links.

:::caution From abroad
You bear the cost of an international transfer yourself. It must not be deducted from the amount that arrives.
:::

## Postal registration {#postal-registration}

The **Postal Registration** card lets you download your documents once the team unlocks them. First check your address and date of birth. If something is missing, add it with **Update Profile**. Then click **Download Documents**. The PDF contains:

- the **binding registration**, which you sign yourself,
- the **declaration of consent from all parents or guardians**, if you are not of legal age when the conference starts. Normally both parents sign it,
- the **declaration of consent for image use**, signed by you and, for minors, by your parents too.

Print the documents, sign them and send the originals by post to the address on the page. A scan or an electronic signature is not enough.

## Committee assignment {#committee-assignment}

If your delegation represents a country, you decide yourselves who sits in which committee. The head delegate sees the **Committee Assignment** card in the quick links for this, as long as some members have no committee yet.

The page lists the committees with your delegation's seats at the top and every member below. Pick a committee for each member and click **Save**. Full committees can no longer be picked.

:::warning Final
Saved assignments cannot be changed. Agree on them in your delegation first.
:::

The other members can only view the page and see **Not yet assigned** until then. Delegations of a non-state actor sit in no committee and need no assignment.

## Conference information {#conference-information}

Whatever the team wants you to know is under **Announcements**, right below the conference header. You only see what is meant for you, for example for your committee or for supervisors.

- **New** marks what you have not read yet. **Mark as read** removes the mark.
- Pinned announcements always stay on top.
- Expand long announcements with **Show more**.
- Open attachments with the buttons below the text.
- The dashboard shows the newest ones. **Show all announcements** leads to the full list.

An **urgent** announcement also shows as a red bar on every page of the conference until you have read it. For some announcements you also get a mail with the full text.

The quick links also hold:

- **Preparation:** the conference's preparation guide, if the team has linked one.
- **Seats of the Conference:** every committee and agenda item, country and non-state actor.

## Schedule {#schedule}

Once the team switches the schedule on, the **Schedule** card appears. Right on the dashboard it shows what is happening now and what comes next. A click opens the conference's whole schedule, day by day. Some sessions are only for those who picked a particular answer in a survey.

## Surveys {#surveys}

Under **Surveys / Polls** you register for events, for example, or the team asks for important information. Choose an answer with **Take Survey** and change it with **Change answer** until the deadline. Fully booked answers can no longer be picked. Open surveys also show up under **Personal Status**.

## Messages {#messages}

Once the team has switched messages on you find the card "Messages" on your dashboard. It shows which chats hold something unread.

- **Trusted persons.** When you want to talk about something in confidence you write to the trusted persons. You reach them at any time, all of them together or one person by name. Only the trusted persons read these messages. When you write to one of them only that person reads it. Nobody else on the team reads along. The trusted persons see your role. You give your name yourself if you want to.
- **Team.** You reach participant care at any time. Once the roles are released the chairs and the committee consultants of your committee join them. Everyone answering an inbox reads along. An answer carries the function and the name of whoever writes it.
- **Other delegates.** With "New message" you search by country, committee or role, for example "fran ga". You only see each other's role, never the name.
- **Your delegation.** Every delegation has a group for all its members.

Chats with other delegates and the delegation group exist only once the roles are released.

When a message stays unread for a quarter of an hour you get an email with a link to your chats. The mail does not contain the message. You turn these mails off below the chat list.

:::tip When something is not right
Before your first message the app shows you the terms of the message service. They set out respectful conduct and explain who can read along. Your message only goes out once you agreed. That holds once for your account. You can read them again at any time with **Read the terms** below your chats.

When you open a chat with unread messages, the line **New messages** shows where you left off.

The three dots at the top of a chat let you block a person or report the chat. After a report participant care reads the chat until the report is resolved. The other person is not told who reported.
:::

## Your school {#school}

Everyone gives their own **School / Institution**, within a delegation too. During registration the field is under **Information and Motivation**, for supervisors under **Your details**. With **Change school** you pick an existing school or enter a new one. It is optional.

## At the conference {#at-the-conference}

During preparation the dashboard reminds you to set up MUNify DELEGATOR on your phone: sign in, stay signed in and add it to your home screen. Wi-Fi at the venue is often scarce.

- **Digital Nametag:** this card shows who you are and which role you have, with a code the team scans. Show it when you ask participant care something.
- **Check-in:** scan the QR code at the entrance with your phone. The page tells you which **Table** to go to. If something is still open for you, such as the payment or your guardians' consent, it says **Please go to participant care** with what is missing. After the scan the page shows **Already checked in**.

## After the conference {#certificate}

Once the conference is over, the dashboard shows your role and the **Certificate of participation**. **Download certificate** gives you the PDF. This only works if you attended the conference. If it says **Certificate not yet available. Please come back later.**, check back later. If the team has uploaded resolutions, you can download them under **Adopted resolutions**.

## For supervisors {#supervisors}

As a supervisor you follow how your students are doing. For privacy reasons you cannot add them yourself: they connect with you. How to register is described in [Registering as a supervisor](./registration#registering-as-a-supervisor).

- **Connect with students:** your link with its QR code, and your invitation code. Copy the link with **Copy Link** or let students scan the QR code. Whoever opens it lands right at **Supervisors** on their own dashboard with the code filled in, and only confirms with **Connect**. **Rotate Code** gives you a new code, and the old link stops working.
- **Your details:** your **Own presence at the conference** and your school. Whether you attend yourself can only be changed during registration. If you attend, you pay the participation fee too.
- **Delegations** and **Single Participants:** every application of your students. During registration you also see the delegations' entry codes. Anyone who has not added you shows up as **Hidden Member**.

After the deadline you see which of your students take part in the selection process. Until the release it then says **The assignment is still in progress**. After that, the **Delegation Statistics** show how your students are doing and the **Personal Status** section shows your own steps, until everyone has paid. Through **Fee Payment** you can also pay for your students. If a committee assignment is still missing in one of your delegations, the dashboard points it out. **Rejected Participants** lists who did not get a seat. If nobody was accepted, you see **Application Rejected**. After the conference you find your own **Certificate of participation** here and how many of your students were accepted.
