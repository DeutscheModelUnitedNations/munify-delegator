# Your conference dashboard

A conference's dashboard holds everything about your participation: your application, your role, the formalities still open and, at the end, your certificate. What you see depends on how far along the conference is and how you take part (in a delegation, as a single participant or as a supervisor).

:::tip Guided tour
The help button (the question mark in the header) offers a guided tour of this page.
:::

## At a glance {#overview}

The conference header with its title, dates and stage is always at the top, followed by the team's **Announcements**. Below them, the content changes with the stage:

- **Registration:** your application, your delegation's members, your delegation preferences and your texts.
- **After the deadline:** the notice **Selection process in progress**, then **The assignment is still in progress** until the team releases the assignment.
- **Preparation:** your role, the **Quick Links** with payment, postal registration and digital nametag, your **Personal Status** and the surveys.
- **During the conference:** the same, plus the check-in at the entrance.
- **After the conference:** your **Certificate of participation**.

If you are not taking part in the conference yet, you see the application instead. How that works is described in [Registration](./registration#choosing-how-to-take-part).

If your age does not fit the conference's age limits, **About your age** sits above the sections of your participation until the conference is over. If your application has not been sent yet, it cannot be sent like this. Otherwise the team will look into it and get in touch if needed.

## While registration is open {#while-registration-is-open}

### Your application

The **Your application** section shows what is still open and the **Application deadline**. Clicking an open task jumps to the section where you do it. In a delegation there are four tasks:

- **Invite people:** a delegation needs at least 2 members.
- **Enter delegation preferences:** at least 3 countries or non-state actors.
- **Answer questionnaire:** the questions about motivation and experience.
- **Complete the profiles and fit the age range:** for everyone applying with the application. What can be missing is explained under [What holds an application back](./registration#application-blockers).

Once all are done, the head delegate completes the application with **Complete registration and participate in the selection process**. After that, nothing can be changed.

:::warning Complete it in time
Only completed applications take part in the selection process. Having created a delegation is not enough.
:::

### Delegation members and entry code {#delegation-members}

Under **Delegation Members** you see every member and the auto-generated **Delegation Name**. Mention this name when you talk to participant care.

As long as the application is not completed, this is also where the entry code is, which others use to join your delegation. Next to it you can:

- **Copy code** or **Copy join link** to pass either on,
- **Copy supervisor link** so your supervisors can register,
- **Show QR code** so others can scan the link directly. In the dialog, **Download QR code** saves it as an image,
- **Rotate Code** (head delegate only) if the code got into the wrong hands. The old code stops working.

What joining looks like from the other side is described in [Joining a delegation](./registration#joining-a-delegation).

### The head delegate

Whoever creates the delegation is its head delegate at first. Only the head delegate can edit the preferences and the texts and complete the application. Until then they can also **Make Head Delegate** or **Remove member** for any member. Both ask first. Whoever hands over the lead becomes a regular member. Everyone else sees the same sections but can only read them.

### Delegation preferences

Under **Delegation Preferences** the head delegate opens the choice with **Set Preferences**:

1. The **Nations Pool** shows for every country which committees it has a seat in and how many seats it has in total. **Non-State Actors** lists the non-state actors.
2. Only roles with at least as many seats as your delegation has members are offered. If your delegation is too big for every role, it says **Delegation has too many members**. The only fix then is to remove members.
3. What you add goes to **Your Preferences**. Change the order with the arrows, with your favourite role on top. Give at least 3 preferences.

If someone joins later, preferences that no longer have enough seats are dropped.

### Information and motivation

Here you answer why your delegation is applying and how much experience you have with Model United Nations. Above that, everyone gives their own **School / Institution** (see [Your school](#school)).

### Supervisors

If you take part with supervisors, such as a teacher, you see them under **Supervisors** with their name and email address. With **Connect Supervisor** you enter their invitation code, check the data shown and click **Connect**. Only participant care can undo the connection. If you opened a link from your supervisor, the dashboard scrolls to this section and highlights it. The code is already filled in, so you only check the data and click **Connect**. The section stays on the dashboard during preparation too.

### Danger Zone

Until the application is completed you can **Leave Delegation** here (if there is more than one of you). The head delegate can also **Delete Delegation**. Your delegation ID is here too, in case support asks for it. What these actions do is explained under [Withdrawing an application](./registration#withdrawing-an-application).

### As a single participant

If you apply alone for an individual role, your dashboard looks similar. Your tasks are **Apply for one (or more) roles**, **Answer questionnaire** and **Complete the profiles and fit the age range**. Under **Role Applications** you add more roles with **Add another application**. In the **Danger Zone** you can withdraw with **Delete all applications**. You complete the application yourself. More in [Applying as a single participant](./registration#applying-as-a-single-participant).

## After applying {#after-applying}

Once the application deadline has passed, the dashboard shows **Selection process in progress**. Below it is either **Application completed** (you take part in the selection process) or **Application not completed** (you cannot be considered). A completed application does not guarantee a seat. Your application's ID for support is shown below.

When the team has handed out the seats but not released them yet, you see **The assignment is still in progress**. Only with the release do you learn which role you get. Depending on the conference's settings you then get an acceptance or a rejection by email, so there is no need to ask.

![Dashboard while the team assigns the seats](shot:participant/dashboard-assignment-pending)

### Not accepted {#not-accepted}

If your application did not get a seat, you see **Application Rejected**. While the conference is in preparation, you can put yourself on the waiting list below it with **Join the waiting list now**. Your previous application has expired. Seats from the waiting list are given out first come, first served. More in [The waiting list](./registration#the-waiting-list).

## During preparation {#preparation}

With the release you see whom you represent. This is a head delegate's dashboard with everything unlocked:

![A head delegate's dashboard during preparation](shot:participant/dashboard-preparation)

- **Quick Links:** cards for everything there is to do now. Cards marked **!** still need something from you. Greyed out cards have not been unlocked by the team yet.
- **Personal Status:** your formal steps: **Registration**, **Fee Payment**, **Binding Registration**, for minors the **Parental Agreement**, the **Media Usage Agreement** and open surveys. You cannot take part without all of them. Each step is **Completed**, **Pending**, **Problem** or **Not unlocked yet**. It stays pending until participant care has ticked it off. For **Problem**, contact participant care if they have not already reached out to you.
- **Delegation Status:** the country or non-state actor your delegation represents, the committees you sit in, the number of members and the delegation name.
- **Delegation Members:** every member with their committee, payment and registration status.
- **Supervisors:** your supervisors, and you can still connect one.
- **Information on your country** or **Information on your NSA**.

As a single participant you see your **Role** instead of the delegation status and members.

Once the team has set them up, the quick links also hold:

- **Digital Nametag:** see [Digital nametag](#nametag).
- **Messages:** see [Messages](#messages).
- **Schedule:** see [Schedule](#schedule).
- **Paper Hub:** where you submit position, working and introduction papers and read the submitted papers of the conference. How it works is explained under [Paper Hub](./paper-hub).
- **Link to Submit Papers:** a form outside DELEGATOR for submitting papers, if the team has set one.
- **Preparation** and **Seats of the Conference:** see [Conference information](#conference-information).

While the conference is being prepared, a notice asks you to set up MUNify DELEGATOR on your phone (see [At the conference](#at-the-conference)). **Done, hide** hides it on this device.

## Payment {#payment}

The **Fee Payment** card opens the payment page once the team unlocks payments. Depending on the conference's settings you then get an email. You pay the participation fee by bank transfer:

1. If you are in a delegation or a supervisor, **Pay for others too** lets you cover several people with one transfer and select them under **Who are you paying for?**. Anyone who has already paid is listed with **(already paid)** and cannot be selected.
2. Check whom the transfer pays for and click **Generate Transaction Reference**. The selection is fixed from then on.
3. Transfer the amount with exactly the **Reason** shown. Scanning the **GiroCode** with your banking app is easiest. Otherwise copy the fields rather than typing them.

This reference is the only way the team can match your payment. If someone in your selection has paid in the meantime, DELEGATOR refuses the reference, so select again. Once your payment has arrived and the team has ticked it off, the page says **Your fee is paid**, the card disappears from the quick links, and depending on the conference's settings you get a confirmation by email. You can then no longer create references, not even for others.

:::caution From abroad
Check every detail especially carefully and use the right currency. You bear the cost of an international transfer yourself. It must not be deducted from the amount that arrives. If you cannot make a SWIFT transfer, contact participant care first.
:::

## Postal registration {#postal-registration}

The **Postal Registration** card lets you download your documents once the team unlocks them. Depending on the conference's settings you then get an email.

1. Check your address and date of birth. If something is missing, the download is locked. Add it with **Update Profile**.
2. Click **Download Documents**. The PDF contains:
   - the **binding registration**, which you sign yourself,
   - the **declaration of consent from all parents or guardians**, if you are not of legal age when the conference starts. Normally both parents sign it,
   - the **declaration of consent for image use**, signed by you and, for minors, by your parents too.
3. Print the documents, fill them in, sign them and send the originals by post to the address on the page. Mind the postage when sending from abroad.

A scan or an electronic signature is not enough. You should read the terms and conditions at the end of the PDF, but you need not print them.

Once your documents have arrived complete, participant care ticks off the steps in your **Personal Status**, and depending on the conference's settings you get a confirmation by email. If something is wrong with a document or the payment, the step shows **Problem**, and you may get an email saying which document is affected. You sort out the details with participant care.

Supervisors who do not attend in person send no documents of their own. The page tells them so.

## Committee assignment {#committee-assignment}

If your delegation represents a country, you decide yourselves who sits in which committee. The head delegate sees the **Committee Assignment** card in the quick links for this, as long as some members have no committee yet.

1. At the top are the committees with your delegation's seats and how many of them are taken.
2. Below, pick a committee for each member. A second click takes the pick back. Full committees can no longer be picked.
3. Once every member has a committee, click **Save**.

:::warning Final
Saved assignments cannot be changed. Agree on them in your delegation first.
:::

The other members can only view the page and see **Not yet assigned** until then. Delegations of a non-state actor sit in no committee and need no assignment.

## Conference information {#conference-information}

Whatever the team wants you to know is under **Announcements**, right below the conference header. You only see what is meant for you, for example for your committee, for minors or for everyone whose payment is still open.

- **New** marks what you have not read yet. **Mark as read** removes the mark, **Mark all as read** does so for all of them at once.
- Pinned announcements always stay on top.
- Expand long announcements with **Show more**.
- Open attachments with the buttons below the text.
- The dashboard shows the pinned, the unread and the newest ones. The link below them, such as **Show all 5 announcements**, leads to the full list.

An **urgent** announcement also shows as a red bar on every page of the conference until you have read it. **Read** opens it, and the cross marks it as read and hides the bar. For some announcements you also get a mail with the full text.

The quick links also hold:

- **Preparation:** the conference's preparation guide, if the team has linked one.
- **Seats of the Conference:** every committee and agenda item, country and non-state actor.

## Schedule {#schedule}

Once the team switches the schedule on, the **Schedule** card appears. Right on the dashboard it shows what is happening now on your track and what comes next. A click opens the conference's whole schedule, day by day. Clicking a session shows its details, with the place, directions and, where there is one, the site plan. Some sessions are only for those who picked a particular answer in a survey.

## Surveys {#surveys}

Under **Surveys / Polls** you register for events, for example, or the team asks for important information. You see the surveys once you have a seat.

1. Click **Take Survey** and choose an answer. Every answer shows how many of its seats are taken. Fully booked answers can no longer be picked.
2. Click **Save**. If the answer has filled up in the meantime, DELEGATOR refuses it with **Selection could not be confirmed because the maximum number has already been reached.**

Until the deadline you change your answer with **Change answer** or take it back with **Withdraw answer**. After that the survey is closed. Open surveys also show up under **Personal Status**. If you do not answer by the deadline, the team can assign you an answer. Depending on the conference's settings you get an email about a new survey and a reminder a few days before its deadline.

## Messages {#messages}

Once the team has switched messages on, you find the **Messages** card in the quick links. It shows which chats hold something unread. When you have unread messages, the header also shows a message icon with their number. Messages are for delegates and single participants, not for supervisors.

- **Trusted persons.** When you want to talk about something in confidence you write to the trusted persons, if the team has named any. You reach them at any time, all of them together or one person by name. Only the trusted persons read these messages. When you write to one of them only that person reads it. Nobody else on the team reads along. The trusted persons see your role. You give your name yourself if you want to.
- **Team.** You reach participant care at any time. Once the roles are released the chairs and the committee consultants of your committee join them. If you have no committee, for example as a non-state actor or single participant, you reach the chairs and consultants of every committee. Everyone answering an inbox reads along and sees who you are. An answer carries the function and the name of whoever writes it.
- **Other delegates.** With **New message** you search by country, committee or role, for example "fran ga". You only see each other's role, never the name.
- **Your delegation.** Every delegation has a group for all its members.

Chats with other delegates and the delegation group exist only once the roles are released. Until then the page tells you that you reach participant care there. Sent messages cannot be changed or deleted.

When a message stays unread for a quarter of an hour, the conference can send you an email with a link to your chats. The mail does not contain the message. You turn these mails off below the chat list at **Email about unread messages**.

:::tip When something is not right
Before your first message the app shows you the **Terms of the message service**. They set out respectful conduct and explain who can read along. Your message only goes out once you agreed with **Agree and send**. That holds once for your account and every conference. If the terms change, the app asks again. You can read them again at any time with **Read the terms** below your chats.

The three dots at the top of a direct chat let you use **Block person** so you can no longer write each other, or **Report chat** to report it. After a report participant care reads the chat until the report is resolved. The other person is not told who reported. Whoever breaks the rules can be suspended from chats with other participants. They can still reach the team. In an emergency call 112 and turn to the team on site directly, since messages are not read around the clock.
:::

## MUNify CHASE {#chase}

If the conference's committees work with MUNify CHASE, the **MUNify CHASE** card appears about ten days before the conference starts. CHASE runs the speakers' list, the votes, the attendance and the resolutions.

- **Open my committee in CHASE** (as a delegate with a committee) or **Open the conference in CHASE** takes you there. You sign in there with the same account.
- **Manual for my role** opens the matching guide in CHASE.
- As a non-state actor, chairs check you in and out of committees with your digital nametag or your printed card. **Open my digital nametag** takes you there.

Depending on the conference's settings you get an email at the same time, inviting you to look around CHASE.

## Your school {#school}

Everyone gives their own **School / Institution**, within a delegation too. During registration the field is under **Information and Motivation**, for supervisors under **Your details**. With **Change school** you pick an existing school or enter a new one. It is optional and can be changed at any time. The assignment does not change it.

## At the conference {#at-the-conference}

During preparation the dashboard reminds you to set up MUNify DELEGATOR on your phone: sign in, stay signed in, add the page to your home screen and charge your phone before each conference day. Wi-Fi at the venue is often scarce. During the conference a short reminder stays until you hide it. Depending on the conference's settings the same request comes by email about a week before.

### Digital nametag {#nametag}

The **Digital Nametag** card in the quick links shows who you are: your country or role with its flag and committee, your name, a QR code and your user ID. Show it at the check-in and when you ask participant care something, so the team can identify you quickly and securely. Turn up the screen brightness for it.

The QR code is signed and only counts for a few minutes. While the page is open it renews the code by itself, also when you bring the page back to the front. A screenshot is therefore no use as a nametag. Without a connection the last code stays until it is too old. If you have no seat yet, the page shows **Not Registered**.

### Check-in {#check-in}

Scan the QR code at the entrance with your phone. It opens your ticket:

- If everything is settled, its top part tells you which **Table** to go to, and for which initial letters of the nations it is. Press, individual roles and non-state actors have a table of their own.
- If something is still open for you, it says **Please go to participant care** with what is missing, for example **Payment is still open**, **Binding registration is still open** or **Under 18, the guardian's consent is missing**. Without a seat it says **You have no seat assigned yet.**
- The bottom part holds your QR code, which the team scans. For an hour afterwards the page shows **Already checked in** with the time of the scan.

If you lose your access badge and someone hands it in at participant care, they send you an email so you can pick it up there.

## After the conference {#certificate}

Once the conference is over, the dashboard shows your role and the **Certificate of participation**. **Download certificate** gives you the PDF. This only works if you attended the conference. Otherwise the dashboard tells you that you cannot download a certificate, and participant care helps if that is a mistake. If it says **Certificate not yet available. Please come back later.**, the team has not uploaded the template yet. Depending on the conference's settings you get an email once the certificate is ready.

The certificate names the sessions you were checked in at and carries a QR code. Whoever scans it sees on a public page whether the certificate is genuine (see [QR codes and check pages](./account#qr-codes)).

If the team has uploaded resolutions, you can download them under **Adopted resolutions**.

## For supervisors {#supervisors}

As a supervisor you follow how your students are doing. For privacy reasons you cannot add them yourself: they connect with you. How to register is described in [Registering as a supervisor](./registration#registering-as-a-supervisor).

- **Connect with students:** your link with its QR code, and your invitation code. Copy the link with **Copy Link**, save the QR code with **Download QR code** or let students scan it. Whoever opens it lands right at **Supervisors** on their own dashboard with the code filled in, and only confirms with **Connect**. **Rotate Code** gives you a new code, and the old link stops working.
- **Overview:** how many delegations, delegation members and single participants you supervise.
- **Your details:** your **Own presence at the conference** and your school. Whether you attend yourself can only be changed during registration. If you attend, you pay the participation fee too and send documents of your own.
- **Delegations** and **Single Participants:** every application of your students, with names and email addresses. During registration you also see the delegations' entry codes, preferences and texts. Anyone who has not added you shows up as **Hidden Member**.

After the deadline you see which of your students take part in the selection process. Until the release it then says **The assignment is still in progress**. Depending on the conference's settings you get a single email with the release, listing the role of every student you supervise.

After that the dashboard only lists the students with a seat, with their committee, payment and registration status and the number of their papers. Once postal registration is unlocked, **Download documents for postal registration** gives you each person's documents. The **Delegation Statistics** show how many have paid, sent their documents and submitted papers. The **Personal Status** section shows your own steps if you come along, until everyone has paid.

Once at least one student has a seat, you get the **Quick Links**. Through **Fee Payment** you can also pay for your students and for other supervisors of the same students, and you see the **Paper Hub** from the supervisor's side (see [Paper Hub](./paper-hub)). Messages and surveys are not for supervisors. If a committee assignment is still missing in one of your delegations, the dashboard points it out.

**Rejected Participants** lists who did not get a seat. If nobody was accepted, you see **Application Rejected**. After the conference you find your own **Certificate of participation** here if you attended, and how many of your students were accepted.

## Emails from the conference {#emails}

For many steps the conference can send you an email. Which of them go out automatically, which the team sends by hand and which it skips is up to each conference's team. So rely on the dashboard rather than on the email. Possible emails are:

- **Application:** the code for the head delegate, a confirmation when you join, a note to the earlier members about each new member, the confirmation of the completed application, and a reminder three days before the application deadline while the application is still open.
- **Result:** the acceptance with your role, or the rejection with a pointer to the waiting list, once the assignment is released. Supervisors get an overview of their students.
- **Waiting list:** the confirmation of your entry and, if you move up, your new role.
- **Formalities:** when payment or postal registration is unlocked, when they have arrived, when there is a problem, and two weeks before the conference a reminder of everything still open.
- **Preparation:** new surveys and a reminder three days before their deadline, feedback on your papers, announcements, unread messages, the invitation to MUNify CHASE and, a week before, the request to set up your phone.
- **Supervision:** to supervisors for every newly connected person, to the person about their supervisor.
- **Afterwards:** that your certificate is ready, if you attended.

Several emails of the same kind, such as about several new members, arrive together as one.
