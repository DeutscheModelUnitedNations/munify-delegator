# Your account

One account takes you to every conference. This page covers what does not belong to a single conference: your details, your sign-in and the pages around the conferences. How to apply for a conference is explained under [Registration](./registration).

## Your personal details {#profile}

You find your details under **My Account**: click the circle with your initials at the top right, then **My Account**. When you first sign in, and whenever something is missing, DELEGATOR sends you there with the notice **Complete data**. After **Save** you continue where you were heading.

Under **Personal Data** DELEGATOR asks for:

- **Contact Info:** first and last name, phone number and **Emergency Contacts**. Under **Legal Name**, enter your name as it appears on your ID document. Some conferences need it for access to government buildings. As an emergency contact, give at least one person with name, phone number and email, so the team can reach someone close to you in an emergency.
- **Address:** which fields are needed depends on the country. The address later appears on your documents for the [postal registration](./dashboard#postal-registration).
- **About you:** **Birth Date**, **Gender**, **Pronouns** (optional) and **Diet**. You must be at least 13 years old. Your birth date decides whether you need your guardians' consent for a conference and whether you fit its age limits. Your pronouns appear next to your name, for example in your delegation's list.
- **Newsletter:** see [Newsletters](#newsletters).

Apart from the pronouns, the apartment line and the newsletters, everything has to be filled in, the address as its country requires. If something is missing, you cannot send an application (see [What holds an application back](./registration#application-blockers)), and the next time you sign in you land here again. You change your name and your other details here only, not at the login service.

## Sign-in details {#login-details}

Next to your details, **Login information** lists what you sign in with: **Username**, **E-mail**, **Password**, **Passkeys** and **Authenticator App**, **Backup Codes** once you have set up a passkey or an app, and your **Connected Accounts** if you sign in through another account. Below them are your **User ID** and, if you have any, your **Rights**.

The login service manages these details. The pencil next to a line takes you there and, once you are done, back to **My Account**. A message there confirms the change, for example **Your email address has been updated successfully.**

DELEGATOR takes over a new email address the next time you sign in. Every email from the conferences then goes to that address. If another account in DELEGATOR already uses the address, you land on **Email Address Conflict** instead (see [Sign-in problems](#sign-in-problems)).

To sign out, use **Logout** under the circle at the top right.

## Who sees your details {#who-sees-your-data}

- **You** see all of your details. You do not see participant care's notes about you.
- **Participant care and project management** of the conferences you take part in, or whose waiting list you are on, see all of your details.
- **Your supervisors** see all of your details once you have connected with them.
- **The members of your delegation** see your name and email address, as well as your committee and whether your payment and documents are done. They do not see your phone number or birth date.
- **Whoever reviews your papers** sees your name.
- **Other participants** only see your role in [Messages](./dashboard#messages), never your name.

Beyond that, the team sees what its task needs, for example who you are and what is still open for you when scanning at the entrance.

If a MUNify CHASE instance is linked to DELEGATOR, it receives your name and email address once the assignment is released, so you can work in it.

## Newsletters {#newsletters}

Under **Newsletter** on **My Account** you choose whether to **Receive general emails about the project** and **Receive emails about joining the DMUN team/organization**. Both are optional and off from the start.

- The unsubscribe link in a newsletter takes you to **Unsubscribe Newsletters**. There, **unsubscribe all newsletters** turns all of them off at once. You turn off single newsletters through **Go to profile management**.
- Unsubscribing does not apply to the mailing lists of conferences you are registered for. Contact participant care for those.
- On the **Team Tender List** page (through a link such as `/tt`) you sign up for the team position tenders with **Sign me up** and leave again with **Unsubscribe**.

## Language {#language}

DELEGATOR is available in German and English. Switch the language with the flags in your menu at the top right or at the bottom of every page. Which language a conference is held in is shown on its card in the overview and in the application form.

## Deleting your account {#deleting-your-account}

You cannot delete your account yourself. If you would like to have it deleted in accordance with the GDPR, contact the participant care team of your conference or support@dmun.de. The note about this is at the bottom of **Login information**.

## Your conferences {#conference-overview}

**Conferences** (click the logo at the top left, or **Dashboard** in the menu) lists every conference.

- **Your conferences** come first: every conference you take part in that is not over yet. Each card shows your part there.
- The others follow, grouped by their stage: **Active Conferences**, **Registration open**, **In preparation**, **Upcoming Conferences** and **Past Conferences**. Past conferences are always listed here, including the ones you took part in.
- Each card names the dates, the place and the **Conference Language**. If you can apply or join the waiting list, it says **Sign up**, otherwise **Go to Dashboard**.

## Without signing in {#without-signing-in}

The conference overview, a conference's dashboard and its **Seats of the Conference** are visible without signing in. The dashboard then shows the conference header, the seats and, if linked, the **Preparation** guide, along with the notice **Your participation**: to apply and to see your own status, sign in. Everything else needs you to sign in.

## Sign-in problems {#sign-in-problems}

If something goes wrong while signing in, you land on a page that says what happened. **Try again** or **Try logging in again** starts over, and **Contact support** opens a prepared email to support.

- **Email Address Conflict:** the address of your account at the login service is already used by another account in DELEGATOR. This happens when you create a second account with the same address, or change your address at the login service to one already in use. Change it there to an unused address, or contact support if the other account is yours. Give the **Reference ID** shown at the bottom of the page.
- **Login Cancelled:** you cancelled the sign-in or denied the required permissions. Try again.
- **Login Required:** your session at the login service has expired. Sign in again.
- **Login Service Unavailable:** try again in a few minutes.
- **Login Failed:** the sign-in took too long, could not be verified or the network dropped. Try again.
- **Login Session Expired:** the sign-in took longer than 5 minutes, you refreshed the page during it or opened several sign-in tabs. Sign in again with **Try logging in again**.
- **Invitation for another email address:** an invitation to the team only works for the account of the invited address. Sign in with that account, or ask the team to invite your address.

## QR codes and check pages {#qr-codes}

Some things carry a QR code that a phone's camera opens:

- **Digital nametag:** the code takes the team straight to your details. Anyone who is not on your conference's team has to sign in first and then sees **You don't have access to this page.** They learn nothing about you, not even whether the code is genuine. An expired code shows the team **This code has expired.** Then open your nametag again (see [Digital nametag](./dashboard#nametag)).
- **Postal registration documents:** the code helps participant care match your documents to you.
- **Certificate of participation:** the code takes anyone, without signing in, to a public check page. If the certificate is genuine, it says **The certificate is valid.** with your name, the conference, its dates and, under **Took part in**, the sessions your certificate names. Otherwise it says **The certificate is invalid.** Links from older certificates lead to this page too.
