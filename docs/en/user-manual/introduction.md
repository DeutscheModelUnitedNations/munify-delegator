# Introduction

MUNify DELEGATOR is the registration system for DMUN's Model United Nations conferences. You apply for a conference here, the team hands out the seats to delegations and single participants, and everyone prepares for the conference together: from payment and documents to the schedule.

## Who plays which part {#roles}

In each conference you have exactly one part:

- **Delegation member:** You apply as a group of at least two people for a state or a non-state actor. Whoever founds the delegation becomes its **head delegate**: they invite the others, enter the delegation's preferences and complete the application for everyone. The lead can be handed to another member.
- **Single participant:** You apply on your own for a special role, for example in the conference press.
- **Supervisor:** Teachers or club leaders accompany their students. They see their applications and status and help with the organisation, but are not given a seat themselves.
- **Team:** The team organises the conference. Team members do not apply but join through an invitation, and each has a team role for their task, such as Project Management, Participant Care or Content Lead.

How to register in one of these parts is explained under [Registration](./participant/registration).

## How a conference moves through its stages {#conference-stages}

Every conference goes through five stages. The dashboard groups the conferences by them.

1. **Upcoming:** The conference has been announced, but registration has not opened yet.
2. **Registration open:** You apply and complete your application before the application deadline. After that, the team reviews every completed application in the selection process and hands out the seats.
3. **In preparation:** Once the team has released the assignment, you learn by email whether you got a seat and in which role. With a seat, you prepare on the dashboard. Without one, you can put yourself on the [waiting list](./participant/registration#the-waiting-list).
4. **Running:** The conference is taking place.
5. **Over:** The conference is over, and the dashboard offers things like your certificate of participation.

What the dashboard shows you in each stage is explained under [Dashboard](./participant/dashboard).

## Where to find help {#getting-help}

- **Help button:** The question mark in the header opens **Help**. There, **Manual for this page** takes you straight to the section that explains the page you are on. Where a page has a guided tour, **Take the tour** starts it and walks you through the most important parts of the page.
- **Footer:** **User manual** in the footer also leads to the matching page of this manual.
- **Command palette:** Press Ctrl+K (⌘+K on a Mac) to search for pages and actions, this manual included.
- **Participant care:** If you have questions about your application or participation, the conference's participant care team will help you.
